- [Spring AI reference](https://docs.spring.io/spring-ai/reference/) — model, embedding, vector store, advisors, observability.
- [Spring AI compatibility/getting started](https://docs.spring.io/spring-ai/reference/getting-started.html) — current compatibility and BOM.
- [Spring Framework REST clients](https://docs.spring.io/spring-framework/reference/integration/rest-clients.html) — RestClient, WebClient, HTTP Service Clients.
