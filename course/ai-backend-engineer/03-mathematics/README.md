# Mathematics for AI

The objective is practical mathematical literacy: read equations, check dimensions, reason about uncertainty and optimization, and diagnose model behavior. You do not need to prove every theorem, but do not treat the math as optional magic.

## Order and outcomes

1. [Linear algebra](./Linear-Algebra.md): vectors, matrices, dot products, projections, norms, cosine similarity and dimensions.
2. [Probability and statistics](./Probability-and-Statistics.md): distributions, conditioning, Bayes, variance, sampling, intervals and testing.
3. [Calculus and optimization](./Calculus-and-Optimization.md): derivatives, partial derivatives, chain rule, gradients, loss and descent.
4. Information theory is covered in [Information theory](./Information-Theory.md); later advanced work can add KL-based training derivations and matrix calculus.

For every formula, identify the symbols, units/shapes, assumptions and a numeric example. Check hand calculations with Python/NumPy. See the [AI From Scratch mathematics course](https://github.com/kavinash0310/ai-from-scratch/tree/main/course/mathematics) for the wider textbook path.
